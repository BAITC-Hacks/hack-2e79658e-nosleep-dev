# BesSheshim brand

## Core identity

- **Product name:** BesSheshim
- **Logo styling:** BES SHESHIM
- **Short mark:** BS/5
- **Descriptor:** AI city budget simulator
- **English tagline:** Five decisions. One city.
- **Kazakh tagline:** Бес шешім. Бір қала.
- **Russian tagline:** Решения, из которых строится город.
- **Demo campaign:** «Аким на 5 часов»

## Name usage

Use **BesSheshim** in prose, metadata, product listings, and URLs. Do not use
`Bes Sheshim`, `Bes-Sheshim`, or `BeshSheshim` as alternate spellings.

Use **BES SHESHIM** only as a visual logo treatment. Use **BS/5** where a compact
mark is needed, such as an app icon, favicon, or small social avatar.

## Positioning

BesSheshim lets a person make five city-budget decisions, see their impact on
districts and quality of life, compare scenarios, and understand the tradeoffs
before committing.

The brand should feel civic, confident, locally rooted, and transparent. It is
an understandable decision experience rather than generic government software
or a black-box AI product.
